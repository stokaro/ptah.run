Ptah in practice

24 things Ptah does, recorded as they happened rather than written up afterwards. Every command and every line of output came off a real run; nothing here is staged and nothing is invented. Open one to read it, or press Play to watch it typed.

Two are always on the home page. The rest take turns there, two to a visit.
